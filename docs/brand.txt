METRON

Product Name:
METRON

Meaning:
Derived from the Greek word “metron” — measure, standard, or a means of measurement.

Core Concept:
Measure your academic journey, understand your current position, and keep moving forward.

Primary Tagline:
Measure. Organize. Progress.

Brand Philosophy:
METRON is built around the idea that academic progress becomes easier to manage when it is visible and measurable.

It transforms a student's semester into a structured system of:
- Goals
- Subjects
- Chapters
- Tasks
- Assignments
- Records
- Observations
- Exams
- Progress
- Performance
- History

Core Loop:
PLAN → ACT → TRACK → MEASURE → REFLECT → PROGRESS

Product Positioning:
METRON is an Academic Execution System that helps students organize, track, measure, and manage their entire academic journey.

It is not a teaching platform.
It is not an LMS.
It is not simply a to-do list.

METRON is a personal academic command center.

Core Promise:

Know what has been done.
Know where you stand.
Know what comes next.
